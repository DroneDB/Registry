DroneDB Registry and DroneDB Hub are free for personal use, for teaching at non-profit educational institutions, for research that a non-profit institution does on its own account and publishes, and for a 60-day evaluation. The full terms are in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

## Who needs a commercial licence

Before you use the software in production, you need a commercial licence if you are:

- a company of any size, a sole trader or a freelancer, including for internal use only;
- a government institution, public body, public research organisation or state-owned company (teaching is free);
- anyone who uses Registry for paid work, for clients, or as part of a product or service.

If you use DroneDB on [hub.dronedb.app](https://hub.dronedb.app) or on a dedicated instance that Digipa runs for you, your cloud subscription covers it. You need no separate licence.

## What a commercial licence can include

Each agreement states what it grants. Nothing below is granted unless the agreement says so.

- Production use on an agreed number of installations.
- Hosting for your own clients.
- Your own branding (white label).
- Private changes to the source code, never distributed.
- Redistribution or embedding in your own product (OEM / reseller agreements only).
- Support, updates and an SLA.

A commercial agreement also requires the update check to stay enabled, and gives Digipa the right to verify licensed use. The agreement is public at <https://dronedb.app/legal/registry-commercial-licence>.

## How to get one

- Request a licence: <https://dronedb.app/request-license>. The Registry admin panel also links to it.
- Or write to [support@dronedb.app](mailto:support@dronedb.app) with your company name, the number of installations and how you use Registry.

Prices are on <https://dronedb.app/request-license>.
